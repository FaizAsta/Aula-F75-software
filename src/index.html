Keyboard tools

Restore settings after a reset

Off by default. When on, your settings are written back if the keyboard comes back with different ones, for example after sleep or a restart. When off, connecting changes nothing on the keyboard.

Brightness levels

How many brightness steps the Lighting tab offers. It grows by itself when the keyboard reports a higher level, for example after you raise it with the keyboard's own brightness keys. A number above what your keyboard supports may do nothing.

Reset keyboard

Puts the lighting (effect, brightness, speed, colours), the per-key colours, the light-off timer and any keys you reassigned back to their defaults. On the F75 Max: lighting, response, sleep, key locks and per-key colours. On the F75 HE: lighting and per-key colours (its Hall-effect settings are not touched). The knob setting and the Fn layer's special keys stay as they are. This cannot be undone, so use Export first if you want a copy of your settings.

Video or live wallpaper

Play a video behind the app: MP4 or WebM, or an animated GIF, up to 500 MB. It loops without sound and pauses while the window is hidden, minimized or not in focus (a GIF cannot be paused, so it is hidden then and comes back when you return). The app keeps its own copy, so it still works if you move or delete the original.

Warning: a video plays on the graphics card, so it can use a lot of GPU. The bigger the video, the more it uses: 4K uses the most, 2K (1440p) uses less, and 1080p uses less again. If the app feels slow or the fans get loud, choose a lower-resolution video. The Liquid glass style is the heaviest with a video.

Connection help

  • 2.4G dongle Battery, effects, colours and per-key lighting. The most tested option.
  • USB-C cable Tried automatically. Some units use a cable protocol this app cannot speak. If it stays silent, use the dongle.
  • Bluetooth Battery only, and only if Windows reports it. Windows does not allow lighting commands over Bluetooth.
